HELP AND PRIVACY

Frequently asked questions

How is my age calculated?

Your age is the calendar difference between your date of birth and the chosen date. Months use their real lengths, and leap years are handled automatically.

Can I calculate my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Turn on “Calculate age on another date” and pick any date on or after your birthday. Future dates are allowed and treated as a projection.

Is my date of birth stored or uploaded?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. Your dates are not sent to a server and this tool does not save them.

How are February 29 birthdays handled?

For a birthday on February 29, the tool uses February 28 in years that are not leap years, so a birthday is still counted once each year.
