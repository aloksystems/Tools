GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

How is my age calculated?

Your age is calculated from the calendar difference between your date of birth and the selected date. Total days use calendar dates, so daylight saving changes do not affect the count.

Can I calculate my age on a past or future date?

Yes. Turn on “Calculate age on a specific date” and enter any valid date on or after your birthday.

Is my date of birth stored or uploaded?

No. The calculation runs in your browser. The date you enter is not sent to a server or saved by this tool.

How are leap-day birthdays handled?

For a birthday on February 29, the next birthday countdown uses February 28 in years that are not leap years.
