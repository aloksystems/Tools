GOOD TO KNOW

Assignment Writer questions

Is my text uploaded to a server?

No. Assignment Writer runs entirely in your browser. Your text is not sent anywhere, and saved texts stay in your browser's local storage.

What is the difference between word, phrase, and sentence mode?

Word mode highlights one word at a time, phrase mode groups a chosen number of words, and sentence mode highlights one complete sentence.

Can I change the pace during a session?

Yes. Use the up and down arrow keys to speed up or slow down while a session is open, or pause and adjust the speed in settings.

Can I reuse text later?

Yes. Choose Save text to keep up to six texts in this browser. Open Recent to load or delete a saved text at any time.
