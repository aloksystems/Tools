HELP AND PRIVACY

Frequently asked questions

Where are my customer records saved?

Records are saved in local browser storage on this device. They are not uploaded to a server. If you clear site data or switch browsers/devices, the saved records will not be available.

How do I enter missed deliveries?

Enter dates as YYYY-MM-DD, separated by commas, or use the Sunday shortcut. Duplicate dates are counted once, and dates outside your selected period are ignored.

What does “If no days were missed” mean?

It is the estimated amount for every calendar day in the period at the selected daily quantity and price. “Amount due” subtracts missed delivery dates.

What happens when I share on WhatsApp?

The tool opens WhatsApp with a prepared summary. You can review or edit the message before choosing to send it.
