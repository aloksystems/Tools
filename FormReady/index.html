    Quick answers

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my documents uploaded to a server?

    No. FormReady processes your files in your browser. Files remain in the current page session and are cleared when you clear the workspace or reload.

    Which file types can I use?

    You can add JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF and PDF files. You can add several files at once or drag them into the upload area.

    What does “maximum size” mean?

    It is an upper limit for the output file. FormReady tries to meet it by adjusting image quality and, when configured, dimensions. Each result reports whether the limit was met.

    Can I set different requirements for each document?

    Yes. Set defaults for the whole batch, then open an individual file’s settings to override its format, size, quality or dimensions.

    Can I combine documents into one PDF?

    Yes. Select the application-pack option to combine supported images into an ordered PDF. You can also download processed files separately or together as a ZIP.

    Does it work on a phone?

    Yes. The workspace adapts to smaller screens. Processing speed depends on your device and the size and number of files.
