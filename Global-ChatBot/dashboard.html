About Global Chat

Global Chat is a real-time chat app built on Firebase. Sign in, join the global room, or create a private room and share its Room ID.

  • Live messaging with the global room and private rooms
  • Google, Facebook or email sign-in
  • Presence and typing indicators
  • Message replies, reactions, editing and deleting
  • Light and dark themes that match the rest of the tools
  • Up to 3 private rooms per account
