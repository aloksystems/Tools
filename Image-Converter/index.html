    HELP AND PRIVACY

    Frequently asked questions

    Are my images uploaded?

    No. Conversion happens on your device with JavaScript and the canvas element. Files are never sent to a server.

    Which formats are supported?

    You can add PNG, JPG, WebP, AVIF, BMP, GIF, TIFF, and HEIC files that the browser can open. Output is available as PNG, JPG, WebP, BMP, and AVIF where the browser supports encoding.

    What happens to transparency when converting to JPG?

    JPG has no transparency, so transparent pixels are filled with the background colour you choose. White is used by default. Keep PNG, WebP, or AVIF to preserve transparency.

    Can I convert several images at once?

    Yes. Add multiple images, convert them together, and download each result or all of them in a ZIP file.
