HELP AND PRIVACY

Frequently asked questions

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. Text recognition runs inside your browser and the image is never uploaded. Closing the page clears the preview and the extracted text.

Which languages can be recognised?

This tool uses the English OCR model. It works best on clear, high-contrast images with printed text.

Which image formats are supported?

You can use JPG, PNG, WebP, BMP, and other common image formats that the browser can open.

Why did the extracted text come out imperfect?

Recognition depends on image quality. Sharper photos, straight scans, and larger text usually produce better results.
