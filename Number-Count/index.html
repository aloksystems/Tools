HELP AND PRIVACY

Frequently asked questions

What does the step control do?

The step decides how much the visible jumps add. A step of 5 counts 0, 5, 10, and so on. The animation always finishes exactly on the end number.

What video format will I get?

The tool asks your browser for a supported recording format, usually WebM in Chrome and Firefox or MP4 where available. If recording is not supported, try a recent Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.

Is the sound included in the downloaded video?

No. The optional sound plays only in the live preview. Exported videos are silent.

Is this tool free and private?

Yes. There is no sign-up, and the count and video are generated in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
