HELP AND PRINTING

Frequently asked questions

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. The photo is read, cropped, and rendered entirely in your browser with the canvas element. Nothing is uploaded or stored online.

Which photo sizes are included?

Presets include India passport 35 × 45 mm, US 2 × 2 inch (50.8 × 50.8 mm), Schengen visa 35 × 45 mm, PAN card 25 × 35 mm, and a 35 × 35 mm square. Choose Custom to enter any size from 5 to 200 mm.

How does the white background option work?

It samples the edges of your photo and replaces similar connected colours with white. It is not an AI cut-out, so it works best on plain, evenly lit backgrounds. Always check the preview before printing.

How should I print the A4 sheet?

Print on A4 paper at 100% or actual size — not fit-to-page — with the printer's own margins at zero or none. The sheet is laid out in millimetres at your chosen DPI, so those settings keep the photo dimensions accurate.

Does changing the DPI change the photo size in millimetres?

No. The physical size stays the same while the pixel dimensions change: pixels = millimetres ÷ 25.4 × DPI. A 35 × 45 mm photo is 413 × 531 px at 300 DPI and 827 × 1063 px at 600 DPI.
