HELP AND PRIVACY

Frequently asked questions

Which YouTube links are supported?

Standard watch links, youtu.be short links, Shorts, embed and live URLs, and plain 11-character video IDs are all accepted.

Why is the maximum resolution missing?

YouTube only stores maxresdefault for some videos. When it is not available, the tool hides that size and shows the resolutions that do exist.

Is this thumbnail downloader free and private?

Yes. The page has no sign-up and no server of its own. Thumbnails load directly from img.youtube.com in your browser.

Can I download more than one size at once?

Yes. Use Download all after a video loads. Your browser may ask for permission before saving multiple files.
