Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the conversions?

All conversions use standard international (SI) conversion factors with high-precision arithmetic.

Does this tool send data to a server?

No. All conversions happen locally in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

What unit categories are supported?

Length (meters, feet, inches, etc.), Mass (kg, lbs, oz, etc.), Temperature (C, F, K), and Volume (liters, gallons, cups, etc.).
