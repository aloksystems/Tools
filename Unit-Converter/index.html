    HELP AND PRIVACY

    Frequently asked questions

    Are the conversions accurate?

    The tool uses standard international conversion factors and exact temperature formulas. Results are rounded for display, so very long decimals may be shortened.

    What is the difference between kB and KiB?

    kB, MB, and GB use powers of 1,000 (decimal). KiB, MiB, and GiB use powers of 1,024 (binary). Both sets are available in the Data category.

    Is my input sent to a server?

    No. Every conversion runs in your browser and nothing you type is uploaded or stored.

    Can I convert negative or decimal values?

    Yes. Enter decimals, negative numbers, or scientific notation such as 1.5e3. Updates happen as you type.
