Frequently asked questions

Is this really free and offline?

Yes. Universal Converter is free, and every conversion runs on your own machine. No files are uploaded to any server.

What is the difference between ZIP and EXE?

The Portable ZIP is the complete package — it includes the app and all bundled converters, so it works right after extracting. The EXE is the core executable; for the full range of conversions it needs the bundled tools present.
