    HELP AND PRIVACY

    Frequently asked questions

    How are shift hours calculated?

    Hours are the time between start and end, minus the unpaid break. If the end time is on or before the start time, the shift is counted as overnight.

    Where are my shifts saved?

    Shifts are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. They are never uploaded. Clearing browser data or using a private window can remove them.

    How is pay estimated?

    Estimated pay is net worked hours multiplied by the hourly rate you enter. It does not include tax, overtime premiums, or other deductions.

    How does the weekly summary work?

    The weekly summary adds logged shifts from Monday through Sunday of the current week. Today's total counts only shifts logged with today's date.
