# Alok Systems Apps — visual direction

## Direction

An independent software studio presented with the calm confidence of an editorial product catalogue. The interface uses a deep navy canvas, precise typography, cool blue accents and restrained borders. App artwork is the primary color and visual interest; the site itself stays quiet. The hierarchy and restrained product storytelling take cues from Raycast and Linear, without borrowing their brand assets.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#0d1118`; cards: `#141a24`; raised cards: `#19212e`; text: `#eef2f8`; muted text: `#9aa7b8`.
- Accent: `#83aaff`; primary action: `#376fcf`; soft accent: `#1a2b47`; hairline: `#293444`.
- Typography: system sans stack with a strong, compact display hierarchy; no remote font dependency.
- Spacing: 4px base, with 8/12/16/24/32/48/64/96px steps; max content width 1180px.
- Components: 9–18px radii, fine borders, minimal shadows, visible focus rings, short color/transform transitions.
- Responsive: 12-column desktop grid, 6-column tablet, single-column mobile; navigation collapses to a menu button.

## References consulted

- [Raycast](https://www.raycast.com/) — clear product hierarchy and quiet, focused calls to action.
- [Linear design refresh](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh) — consistent interface rhythm, softer separation and reduced visual noise.
