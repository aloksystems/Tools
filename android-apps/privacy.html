Privacy

A simple, private catalogue.

This static website does not include analytics or advertising scripts.

Theme preference

If you change the theme, the site stores that preference in your browser’s local storage. It is not sent to Alok Systems.

Downloads and external links

APK files are served directly from this website. No account is required, and Alok Systems does not add analytics or tracking to downloads. Links to GitHub or email providers are subject to those services’ own privacy policies.

Hosting

The hosting provider may process standard server logs to deliver this website. Contact the hosting provider for details about its log retention and privacy practices.
