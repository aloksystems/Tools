HELP AND PRIVACY

Frequently asked questions

What does N-up printing mean?

N-up places several source pages on a single sheet. A 2×2 grid prints four pages per sheet, a 3×2 grid prints six, and so on. This saves paper and is handy for handouts, proofs, and compact archives.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. PDFs and images are read and rendered entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing is sent to a server, and closing the tab clears the pages and thumbnails.

Can I reorder or rotate pages?

Yes. Drag any thumbnail to a new position, use the rotate button on a thumbnail, or remove pages you do not need. The preview and sheet count update immediately.

Which paper sizes and grids are supported?

A4, A3, Letter, and Legal in portrait or landscape. Choose presets from 1×1 up to 5×5, or enter any custom grid up to 10×10. Margins and gaps can be adjusted in millimetres.

Why does the printed result look scaled differently?

In the browser print dialog, disable headers and footers and keep the scale at 100% or “actual size”. The page already includes its own margins, so extra printer scaling can crop the grid.
